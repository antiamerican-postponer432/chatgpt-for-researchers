## 📋 Best practices for your research

- Keep your prompts specific. If you ask for code, specify the language and the task.
- Break large projects into smaller tasks. This makes the AI output more accurate.
- Review all mathematical results. Always check the calculations manually to ensure accuracy.
- Save your logs frequently. You can refer to these logs later to remember how you achieved a specific result.
